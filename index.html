How it works

Anything becomes a world you walk through

Atrium turns notes, a chapter or a talk into a journey through your palace: a vivid 3D scene at every stop, each one leading to the next. The order is kept, which flashcards lose.

01

Paste anything

A chapter, a speech, a list, a web page or a PDF. Or just say a topic: “the twelve cranial nerves”.

02

Walk through it

Every stop is a real 3D scene, and anything you name becomes its own object. Write each one with a gesture of your own.

03

Never forget it

Lanterns dim as memories fade. Recall forwards, backwards or straight to number seven, and they bloom back to gold.

The promise

You never fall behind

Most study apps punish a missed week with a mountain of reviews. Atrium does the opposite.

  • Seven minutes, always. Each session picks what is fading most and what matters most to your goals. The rest can wait.
  • A comeback walk, not a backlog. Away for days? You get a short, gentle walk through your palace. The count stays hidden.
  • An ember, not a guilt streak. Freezes cover the busy days, and a missed day can be repaired within 48 hours.

Off the headset

Your palace, in your pocket

The companion app pairs with a six-letter code: build palaces from text, a web page or a PDF, play the daily challenge, and see your friends.

  • Capture anything. Paste notes or a word list. Atrium drafts the scenes; they wait in your headset inbox until you co-create them.
  • Walk it from memory. A mental walk on the bus keeps your ember alive.
  • Bring your AI agent. Give a study assistant its own key over MCP. It can add to your inbox and ask what you already know. Revoke it any time.
